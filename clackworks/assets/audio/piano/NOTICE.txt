Salamander Grand Piano — Alexander Holm

Recordings of a Yamaha C5 piano. Five MP3 files bundled unchanged from the
Tone.js audio mirror; playback trims leading silence, adjusts pitch, filters,
and applies note-off damping. Arrangement and game sound design are original.
No Tone.js runtime is used.

Author's public-domain declaration, dated March 4, 2022:
https://rytmenpinne.wordpress.com/sounds-and-such/salamander-grandpiano/
The author states: "As of 4.3.2022, this is now public domain!"
Verified October 6, 2026.
